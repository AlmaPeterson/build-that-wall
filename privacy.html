Privacy Policy

Build That Wall Online, by Beacon Political. Last updated: [DATE]

What we collect

  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID and first name. We use them only to run your account: your player name, saved stats and looks, your free trial, and what you have bought. We do not keep your email address.
  • Game data: your stats (games, wins, best miles, walls built), your chosen name and looks. Without signing in, this is kept only in your own browser.
  • Purchases: payments are handled by PayPal. We never see your card details. We keep the PayPal order number and what it unlocked.
  • Online games: game moves and player names are sent through a public game relay while you play. Do not use personal information as your player name.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show third-party ads, or share your data except with the services needed to run the game (Google sign-in, PayPal, and our hosting provider).

Deleting your data

Press Delete my saved data in Account & Store. Your account data is removed straight away and any Gold renewal is stopped.

Children

The game is for players aged 13 and over.
