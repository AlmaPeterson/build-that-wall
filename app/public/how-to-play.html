How to Play Build That Wall

The outrageous political strategy card game, free in your browser

Build That Wall is a card game for 2 to 6 players. Everyone races to build the most miles of wall, while rivals take your sections with Debt, send you into Quarantine, and steal your cards. You can learn it in a few minutes.

Ways to play

  • Against bots: play alone against 1 to 5 computer players, each Easy, Medium or Hard.
  • Pass'n'Play: everyone shares one phone, tablet or computer and passes it on after their turn. No peeking!
  • Online: host a game and share the room code with friends, or join theirs.

Nothing to download or install: it runs in any modern web browser.

Goal and setup

  • Goal: have the most miles of wall when the game ends.
  • Players: 2 to 6 (3 or 4 is best). Change the number of players in Settings.
  • Setup: press Start Game. The deck is shuffled and every player is dealt 5 cards. The rest of the deck becomes the Draw pile.
  • The player to the left of the dealer goes first, then play moves clockwise.
  • Play Immediately cards have a red title. You must use them right away: at the start of your first turn, or as soon as you draw one. They don't cost an action.

Taking your turn

  1. Draw 3 cards from the Draw pile. Use any Play Immediately cards you drew.
  2. Take up to 3 actions. You can skip actions if you want. An action is building a wall or playing a card. Most cards cost 1 action, but Play Immediately, Self-Defense, Savings and Wall Section cards do not. Played cards go to the Discard pile unless the card says otherwise. Once a card is played it can't be taken back.
  3. Press End Turn when you are done. The next player draws their cards and their row on the Cards in Hand board turns red. When the Draw pile runs out, the Discard pile is shuffled to make a new one.

Building walls

  • To build, lay a Wall Section from your hand face up in front of you and pay for it with a Man Hour card, or with 2 other Wall Section cards instead. Building uses 1 action.
  • Matching sections stack together: 1 section = 3 miles, 2 sections = 8 miles, 3 sections = 16 miles. 3 matching sections make a complete wall set. A complete set can't be taken or destroyed.
  • Voting Machine works as a Man Hour that builds 2 sections with one action.

Winning and key cards

  • The game ends when a player completes 3 wall sets, or (faster game) when the Draw pile runs out. Then everyone else gets one last turn. Most miles wins!
  • Debt: take a built section from an opponent. They can block it with Savings.
  • Self-Defense: stops a bad effect aimed at you.
  • Trump: discard to draw 3 cards. Trump Train: draw 3 cards, or protect a set of 2.
  • Every other card explains itself. In the online game, tap any card to see what it does.

Strategy tips

  • Build matching sections: a pair is worth 8 miles (not 6), and a set of 3 is worth 16 and can't be taken.
  • Keep a Savings card if you can: it blocks Debt.
  • Use Debt on the leader's pairs and singles. Complete sets are safe.
  • Save Man Hours for when you have a Wall Section that matches what you already built.
  • Watch the Draw pile: in the faster game, the final round starts when it runs out, so build before it's too late.
